Currency
Korean Won (KRW). 1 USD ≈ 1,350 KRW. Cards accepted almost everywhere. Cash needed for traditional markets.
Transportation
Get a T-money card at any convenience store (₩2,500). Works on subway, bus, and taxis. Subway is the cheapest way to travel.
WiFi & SIM
Free WiFi available at most cafes, subway stations, and public areas. Tourist SIM cards available at Incheon Airport.
Free Attractions
Gyeongbokgung, Changdeokgung (Hanbok = free entry), National Museum, War Memorial, most parks and temples are FREE.
Eating Cheap
Look for "착한가격업소" (Budget-Certified) sticker. Gimbap shops, market food, and convenience store meals are great budget options.
